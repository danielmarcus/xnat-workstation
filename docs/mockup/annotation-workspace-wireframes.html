Annotation workspace — wireframes for review

Proposal for the annotation side panel and the segmentation tools after the SegWorks parity work. Tools move out of the side panel; the panel keeps the annotation list and one operation. Each decision below has alternatives; the recommended one is outlined in teal. Default tool sets are an educated guess, to be tuned once real usage is known. October 2026, against algorithm-modules at 00610f0.

At 1280 × 760 the list keeps its share only because the operation shrinks to 189 px and the controls to 175 px — both then scroll.

What the redesign has to fix

  • Four stacked regions that change at different rates — list (occasional), operation (a job), tool grid (often), tool controls (constantly) — all open at once in one 400 px column.
  • Weak section boundaries: 1 px zinc-800 rules between near-identical zinc-900 surfaces.
  • Two pickers for one idea: tools are a 19-button grid, operations a dropdown.
  • Too many tools on show, several of them variants of each other (five brushes, three region growers, two rectangles).
  • Long control lists (Level Trace 13 rows, Seeded 8+) that scroll inside a fixed box.
  • Repeated context: the active member's name in the list, the operation header, the toolbox header and the controls header.

Section chrome (applies to whichever option is chosen)

  • Header bands: 28 px, a surface two steps lighter than the panel (zinc-800 on zinc-900), uppercase label, and full-width rules above and below — instead of today's 1 px rule between identical surfaces.
  • Body surfaces: the operation body sits on its own surface (zinc-850), so the list and the operation differ in tone as well as by a line.
  • Spacing: 8 px vertical rhythm inside bodies; label column fixed at 52 px so controls line up down the operation.
  • Collapsed operation: the band alone plus a one-line summary (“Seeded · GrowCut · 3 + 2 seeds · preview open”).

What this supersedes

  • The frozen annotations-panel mockup's “context-sensitive toolbox at the bottom of the panel”, and CLAUDE.md's description of it (to be updated with the change).
  • D2 and its 12 Oct amendment (toolbox grid in the panel, fixed-height Controls area); D1's Operations section becomes R5's drawer.
  • The rule that annotation lifecycle lives only in the side panel is kept: the rail and options bar choose how to draw, while creating, naming, saving and deleting stay in the panel.

Open questions for you

  • Is merging the five brushes into one Brush with Kernel and Limit (R3-B) acceptable, or should Threshold stay a separate rail button because people reach for it by name?
  • Should ⌘K ship in the first pass or follow it?
  • Random Walker moves into Seeded segmentation in this change (agreed in principle earlier) — confirm.

How it would be built

One branch, in slices that each leave the app working: (1) rail + options bar with today's tools one-for-one, toolbox removed from the panel; (2) panel restructure — header bands, operation drawer, single editing chip; (3) tool merges and moves (R3-B); (4) grouped operations menu and ⌘K. Each slice is checked in the built app with screenshots against these wireframes, and the existing tool E2E suite is ported to drive the rail rather than the grid.
